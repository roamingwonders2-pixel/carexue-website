MedTime App

Medication reminders for patients and the families who care for them

MedTime is a mobile app from Carexue Inc. It reminds patients when each dose is due, keeps a simple record of what was taken, and lets the family caregivers they invite know if a dose is missed.

Android and iPhone · from Carexue Inc

Dose reminders

An alarm at each scheduled time, even on silent.

Missed-dose alerts

Invited family caregivers are told when a dose is missed.

Refill reminders

A heads-up before a medicine runs out.

Health records

Appointments, readings such as blood pressure, and dose history in one place.

Text message and WhatsApp alerts are optional. A patient or caregiver receives them only after entering their own mobile number in the MedTime app and agreeing to receive MedTime alerts. Messages are sent only about the person's own reminders and the patients they look after: dose reminders, missed-dose alerts and refill reminders. We never send marketing messages.

Message frequency varies with the medication schedule. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and SMS & WhatsApp Terms.
