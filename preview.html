MedTime app

Medication reminders for patients and the families who care.

MedTime is a mobile app from Carexue Inc for Android and iPhone. Patients add their medicines and schedule; the app reminds them when each dose is due and keeps a record of what was taken. Family caregivers the patient invites can see how they're doing and are alerted if a dose is missed.

Dose remindersAn alarm at each scheduled time, even on silent.
Missed-dose alertsInvited caregivers are told when a dose is missed.
Refill remindersA heads-up before a medicine runs out.
Health recordsAppointments, readings and dose history in one place.
Text message and WhatsApp alerts are optional. A patient or caregiver receives them only after entering their own mobile number in the MedTime app and agreeing to receive MedTime alerts, and only about their own reminders and the patients they look after — never marketing. Message frequency varies with the medication schedule. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and SMS & WhatsApp Terms.
